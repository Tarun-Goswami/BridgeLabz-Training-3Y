7. Typography and Text Styling

Why Clear Typography Matters

Good typography makes written information easier to follow. Different font families, sizes, and weights create a clear visual order for readers.

Use highlighting for important ideas, strong text for emphasis, and italic text for a different tone. Comfortable line-height and letter spacing improve readability.
